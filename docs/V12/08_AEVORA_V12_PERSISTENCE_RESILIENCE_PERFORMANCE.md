# AEVORA V12 — Persistence, Resilience & Performance

## Persistence
The world continues operating when the Chairman is elsewhere. HQ does not freeze when visiting another company.

## State storage
Persist spatial continuity, coordinates, visual references, navigation/simulation continuity, streaming/cache state and spatial history without replacing Aevora business truth.

## History/replay
Support current state, historical state, snapshots and authorized replay derived from recorded authoritative/world events.

## Failure behavior
Support backend degradation, network loss, reconnection, missed-event recovery and deterministic reconciliation.

Distinguish confirmed, stale, unavailable and reconnecting states. Never invent current truth.

## Performance
Use hierarchical streaming/LOD, animation LOD, physics prioritization, asset streaming, occlusion, VR optimization and context-aware entity priority.

Priority considers Chairman proximity, active conversation/meeting, business importance, emergency state, camera visibility and VR interaction.

## Observability
Trace Aevora event → world projection → navigation → simulation → renderer with correlation IDs, timestamps, versions, metrics and diagnostics.
