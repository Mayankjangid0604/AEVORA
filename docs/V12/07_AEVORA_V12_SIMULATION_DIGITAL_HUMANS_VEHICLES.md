# AEVORA V12 — Simulation, Digital Humans & Vehicles

## Digital humans
Every real person represented in V12 receives persistent digital embodiment with identity, appearance, role context, workspace, vehicle, location and animation profile.

## Behavior
Aevora autonomy determines business intent. V12 provides locomotion, navigation, animation, facial/body embodiment, spatial audio and physical interaction.

## Movement
Support walking, running, sitting, standing, turning, stairs, elevators, doors, meetings, vehicle entry/exit, workplace transitions, travel and emergency movement.

No random activity without enterprise reason.

## Vehicles
Vehicles are first-class world entities with identity, driver, occupants, location, destination, route, parking, maintenance and company assignment.

Chairman travel supports driver, Assistant, convoy, interior/exterior views, route visualization and voice interaction.

## Physics
Separate enterprise intent, world interaction rules, navigation, collision, physical simulation and renderer physics. Enterprise decides **why**; world/physics determines **how**.
