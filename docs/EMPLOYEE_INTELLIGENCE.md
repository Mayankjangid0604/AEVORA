# Phase 12: Advanced Employee Intelligence & Decision Support

## Mission
Phase 12 of AEVORA implements contextual employee intelligence. It provides the foundation for employees (agents) to reason about their work, gather structured evidence, assess risks, plan steps, propose decisions to management, and ask for assistance when blocked.

## Architecture

The system utilizes the following Prisma entities:
- **IntelligenceSession**: Bounded contexts for an agent to evaluate a goal, task, or project.
- **IntelligenceAssessment**: Structured summaries, recommendations, risks, options, and assumptions derived during a session.
- **IntelligenceEvidence**: Supporting data linked to an assessment (e.g. from the knowledge base, database records, etc.).
- **IntelligencePlan & IntelligencePlanStep**: Step-by-step action plans generated by the agent.
- **DecisionProposal**: Formal proposals submitted by the agent for management approval.
- **DecisionOption & DecisionReview**: Alternative options within a proposal and feedback provided by reviewers.
- **AssistanceRequest**: Mechanisms for an agent to escalate questions or blockers.
- **RiskObservation**: Identified risks with severity, likelihood, and mitigation strategies.
- **IntelligenceOutcome**: Evaluation of the session's actual outcome against expected outcomes.

## Agent Work Cycle Integration
The agent's `WorkCycleService` natively supports the intelligence actions:
- `START_INTELLIGENCE_SESSION`
- `ASSESS_SITUATION`
- `PLAN_APPROACH`
- `PROPOSE_DECISION`
- `REQUEST_ASSISTANCE`
- `ASSESS_RISK`
- `RECORD_OUTCOME`
- `COMPLETE_INTELLIGENCE_SESSION`

## Safety & Governance
Phase 12 maintains strict adherence to the fundamental safety principles of AEVORA:
1. **Bounded Intelligence**: Agents operate within defined sessions with clear objectives.
2. **Management Authority**: Agents propose, managers decide.
3. **Auditability**: All reasoning, evidence, and outcomes are stored in the relational database.
4. **Data Isolation**: Intelligence sessions and proposals strictly belong to a specific company ID.
