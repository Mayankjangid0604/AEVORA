FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Is there a separate section for ladies?

Yes! We have a fully separate ladies section with dedicated trainers, AC, and privacy. Morning and evening batches available.

Can I try before joining?

Absolutely. Walk in for a free trial session. Our trainer will guide you through the equipment and help you understand which plan suits you best.

Do you provide diet plans?

Yes. Our Premium and Annual members get diet plan templates. Personal diet consultation is available as an add-on for Basic members.

What equipment do you have?

We have treadmills, cross trainers, rowing machines, power racks, Smith machine, cable crossover, full dumbbell rack (2–50 kg), leg press, and more — 50+ pieces of equipment total.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking is available for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers right outside the gym.
